AI Data Centres

And How They're A Social Issue

Our initiative as a team is to stop the construction and expansion of A.I. data centers. We are doing this to help the environment, quality of life for those around them, and to stop using up crucial drinking water that communities need to function. We are creating digital awareness to this social issue using this landing page as our campaign. You can view our hand-crafted video on this topic of social impact by clicking on the link to the right.
